:::info About these results

See the [test-retest reliability overview](/reliability/overview/) for the image selection, quality control, scaling and ICC methods.

:::

The Hemifields feature set measures biomarkers in the full macula-centered circle and its superior and inferior halves on F2 fundus images. Murray equivalent diameters use the optic-disc-centered regions described with their results.

Hemifields uses the same sampled F2 image pairs as the Macula-centered feature set. Its quality control and RPS calculation use measurements from its own export: full-circle sharpness for blur filtering, and full-circle a* and b* for RPS.
