**TemporalAngle.** On each concentric circle of radius $r$, the two dominant temporal vessels are identified by diameter and spatial continuity. The angle at the disc center is

$$
\theta_r = \angle\big(\overline{OD\,p_1(r)},\, \overline{OD\,p_2(r)}\big),
$$

and the reported value is the median over valid radii, in degrees. By default,
five circles span 2/3 to approximately 0.787 of the disc–fovea distance.
This requires both disc and fovea geometry; spatial calibration does not change
the angle.
