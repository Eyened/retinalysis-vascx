**TemporalAngle** attempts to capture the opening angle of the main vascular arcades. On each concentric circle of radius $r$, the two dominant temporal vessels are identified by diameter and spatial continuity. The angle at the disc center is

$$
\theta_r = \angle\big(\overline{OD\,p_1(r)},\, \overline{OD\,p_2(r)}\big),
$$

and the reported value is the median over valid radii, in degrees.
